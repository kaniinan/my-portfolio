Who is Kaniinan?




As a writer, I am passionate about understanding the world through travel and talking with other people, appeared in both my writing and my art.

To uncover the truths of the world more deeply, data collection and data analysis are kinds of key to the goal. These methods have been applied in my academic research within the fields of Linguistic Landscape and Records Management. Graduating with Language and Technology has paved the way for me to get familiar with several digital tools. For example, I utilize SQL for data management, while a tool like Tableau help me for visualization help me translate complex insights into clear visual narratives.

I am eager to contribute to projects that explore our data-driven world, with a focus on transforming raw information into visually appealing, story-driven insights, or the writing and exploring projects to tell fascinating stories that were discovered!
